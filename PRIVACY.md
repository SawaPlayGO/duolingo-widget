# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This is an unofficial, open source widget for Duolingo. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Duolingo, Inc.

## What the app does with data

- The app asks for your Duolingo username.
- It sends this username in a request to Duolingo's public web endpoint
  (https://www.duolingo.com/2017-06-30/users?username=...) to read your
  public profile data (for example, streak and XP) and display it on the tile.
- The username is stored locally on your device in the app's settings so the
  widget can refresh. It is not sent anywhere else.

## What the app does NOT do

- It does not ask for or store your Duolingo password or login credentials.
- It does not collect analytics or telemetry.
- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party other than Duolingo.

## Third parties

Requests to duolingo.com are subject to Duolingo's own privacy policy:
https://www.duolingo.com/privacy
